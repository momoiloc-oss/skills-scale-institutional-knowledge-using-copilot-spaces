# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Collaborate with **Product Managers** on acceptance criteria and feature specifications
- Work with **Project Managers** on scheduling and dependency management
- Partner with **QA/Testing Lead** on test coverage and quality standards
- Report technical risks and blockers to **Technical Lead/Architect**

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Partner with **Project Manager** on delivery timelines and release planning
- Work with **Product Lead** on strategic prioritization and product direction
- Align with **Stakeholders** on business requirements and success metrics
- Review quality gates with **QA/Testing Lead** before feature acceptance

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Coordinate with **Product Manager** on feature priorities and backlog sequencing
- Work with **Sponsor/Executive** on resource allocation and escalation
- Partner with **Technical Lead/Architect** on technical dependency management
- Escalate blockers to appropriate leadership via defined escalation paths

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Lead owns quality assurance processes, test planning, and acceptance validation. They ensure that features meet quality standards and acceptance criteria before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain quality standards and test strategies
- Create and manage test plans for features and releases
- Conduct acceptance testing and quality validation
- Identify and report defects with clear reproduction steps
- Collaborate on automation and testing frameworks
- Ensure compliance with security and regulatory requirements

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality, reliable features that meet acceptance criteria
- Reduce defects and rework through proactive testing
- Maintain test coverage and continuous quality monitoring
- Enable fast, confident releases

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement sessions
- Daily standup updates on test status and blockers
- Quality gate reviews and release readiness assessments
- Defect and test coverage reports

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Collaborate with **Developers** on test coverage, automation, and quality gates
- Review acceptance criteria with **Product Manager** to ensure testability
- Coordinate release readiness with **Project Manager**
- Report quality metrics and risks to **Sponsor/Executive** when necessary
- Work with **Technical Lead/Architect** on testing strategy and infrastructure

---

## Product Lead

### Role Summary
Product Lead owns the product strategy, vision, and high-level prioritization across multiple projects or initiatives. They work closely with the Product Manager to ensure alignment with business goals and set the strategic direction.

### Responsibilities
- Define product strategy and long-term vision
- Set high-level priorities across the roadmap
- Make strategic trade-off decisions between features and initiatives
- Align product decisions with business goals and stakeholder needs
- Review and approve major product changes and releases
- Mentor Product Managers on strategy and decision-making

### Goals
- Maximize long-term product impact and market position
- Ensure strategic alignment across all product initiatives
- Balance business, customer, and technical priorities
- Enable Product Managers to make empowered, data-driven decisions

### Typical Communication
- Monthly strategic reviews and roadmap planning
- Ad-hoc alignment with stakeholders on product direction
- Escalation authority for prioritization conflicts
- Quarterly business reviews and strategic planning sessions

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Partner with **Product Manager** on roadmap prioritization and feature validation
- Work with **Project Manager** on release planning and timeline trade-offs
- Align with **Sponsor/Executive** on business goals and resource allocation
- Review technical feasibility with **Technical Lead/Architect**
- Escalate blockers and risks through defined paths

---

## Stakeholders

### Role Summary
Stakeholders are representatives from business, customer-facing, or technical teams who have a vested interest in the project outcomes. They provide requirements, feedback, and approval authority.

### Types of Stakeholders
- **Business Stakeholders**: Sales, marketing, finance teams concerned with revenue, market fit, and business metrics
- **Customer Stakeholders**: Customer success, support, or customer advisory board representatives who advocate for end-user needs
- **Technical Stakeholders**: Platform, infrastructure, or security teams who need alignment on technical decisions and dependencies

### Responsibilities
- Provide business and customer requirements and priorities
- Review and approve major feature and release decisions
- Share feedback on product direction and user needs
- Support communication and adoption of new features
- Escalate business-critical issues and dependencies

### Goals
- Ensure product delivers measurable business value
- Maintain alignment between product, customer, and business needs
- Enable smooth adoption and support of new features
- Reduce risk of misaligned delivery

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates and demos
- Quarterly business reviews
- Ad-hoc requirement and feedback sessions
- Escalation calls for business-critical issues

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Provide input to **Product Manager** on business requirements and success metrics
- Review releases and roadmap with **Product Lead**
- Receive status updates and risk reports from **Project Manager**
- Report blockers and risks to **Sponsor/Executive** when needed
- Validate feature acceptance with **QA/Testing Lead**

---

## Sponsor/Executive

### Role Summary
Sponsor/Executive provides executive oversight, resource allocation, and escalation authority. They ensure projects align with organizational strategy and have the resources needed to succeed.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project initiation and charter
- Allocate budget and team resources
- Make strategic trade-off decisions on priorities and scope
- Escalate and resolve major blockers
- Communicate project status to upper leadership
- Review project outcomes and business impact

### Goals
- Maximize return on investment for product initiatives
- Ensure strategic alignment with organizational goals
- Enable teams to deliver with clear ownership and resources
- Reduce risk of scope creep and resource constraints

### Typical Communication
- Monthly project status and risk reviews
- Approval gates at project initiation and release
- Ad-hoc escalation and decision-making
- Quarterly executive reviews and strategic planning

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Review project charter and roadmap with **Product Lead**
- Receive risk and status reports from **Project Manager**
- Allocate resources to **Developers**, **QA/Testing Lead**, and other team members
- Approve major scope and timeline decisions with **Product Manager**
- Escalate business-critical risks to organizational leadership

---

## Technical Lead/Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Lead/Architect owns technical design, architecture decisions, and technical governance. They ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with organizational technical strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical design and architecture for features
- Review code and ensure adherence to technical standards
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and dependencies
- Mentor developers on best practices and technical decisions
- Manage technical debt and refactoring priorities
- Approve integration points and platform changes

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, well-architected solutions
- Reduce technical risk and technical debt
- Enable fast, confident delivery through good architecture
- Maintain system performance, reliability, and security

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review and technical governance sessions
- Sprint planning for technical risk assessment
- Technical risk escalation and mitigation planning

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Collaborate with **Developers** on design and technical best practices
- Review feasibility with **Product Manager** and **Product Lead**
- Report technical risks and dependencies to **Project Manager**
- Coordinate with **QA/Testing Lead** on testing strategy and infrastructure
- Escalate technical blockers and architectural decisions to **Sponsor/Executive** when necessary
- Partner with **Security/Compliance Officer** on security architecture

---

## Security/Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security/Compliance Officer owns security review, compliance requirements, and incident response. They ensure features and releases meet security and regulatory standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define security requirements and standards
- Review features for security and compliance risks
- Conduct security testing and vulnerability assessments
- Manage security incident response and escalation
- Ensure compliance with relevant regulations and policies
- Provide security guidance to product and engineering teams

### Goals
- Deliver secure, compliant features that protect customer and company data
- Reduce security risk and vulnerability exposure
- Enable fast, secure releases through proactive security review
- Maintain regulatory compliance and audit readiness

### Typical Communication
- Security review gates before feature acceptance and release
- Incident response and escalation
- Compliance and audit reporting
- Security training and guidance sessions

### Interactions with Other Roles
- Review features with **Developers** and **Technical Lead/Architect** for security risks
- Coordinate security testing with **QA/Testing Lead**
- Provide compliance and security requirements to **Product Manager**
- Escalate security risks to **Sponsor/Executive** and incident response team
- Report security metrics and compliance status to organizational leadership
